## Full description

**HMRC Mileage Claim Pack 2026/27** — printable mileage log, MAR how-to, and Approved Mileage Allowance Payment (AMAP) rates for tax year **6 April 2026 – 5 April 2027**.

Built for UK employees and sole traders who use their own car, van, motorcycle or bike for work and need tidy records. Employees who are paid below the HMRC approved rates may be able to claim **Mileage Allowance Relief (MAR)** on the shortfall — this pack explains the idea in plain English and gives you templates to keep evidence. It does **not** file anything with HMRC.

### What’s included
- Printable mileage log sheets (date / from / to / purpose / miles)
- MAR how-to (when it may apply; shortfall-only; passenger shortfalls don’t qualify)
- 2026/27 AMAP rates card (cars/vans **55p** first 10,000 business miles, then **25p**; motorcycles **24p**; bicycles **20p**; passenger **+5p**)
- Instant digital download
