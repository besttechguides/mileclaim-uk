# Mileage Allowance Relief (MAR) — Plain English How-To  
**Tax year 2026/27 · MileClaim UK**

> **Loud and clear: this is NOT tax advice.**  
> It is a short overview of HMRC’s published rules. Your situation may differ. Check [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/tax-relief-for-employees/vehicles-you-use-for-work) or speak to a qualified adviser before claiming.

---

## When does MAR apply?

**Mileage Allowance Relief** may be available if you:

1. Use **your own** car, van, motorcycle or bicycle for **business** journeys (not usually ordinary home-to-work commuting), **and**
2. Your employer pays you **less** than the HMRC **Approved Mileage Allowance Payment (AMAP)** rates for those miles — or pays you nothing.

If your employer already pays at (or above) the approved rates for those miles, there is typically **no** MAR to claim on those miles.

---

## Important: relief is on the *shortfall*, not the full AMAP

MAR is **not** “cash equal to 55p × every mile.”

It is tax relief on the **difference** between:

- what HMRC’s approved amount would be for your business miles, **minus**
- what your employer already paid you as mileage allowance for those miles.

Example idea: if the approved amount is £550 and your employer paid £450, the shortfall is **£100** — relief relates to that shortfall (through lower taxable income), not a £550 cheque.

## How people usually claim

| Situation | Typical path |
|-----------|----------------|
| Employee, not in Self Assessment for other reasons | Often **form P87** (claim tax relief on employment expenses) if you meet HMRC’s criteria |
| Already complete a tax return | Claim via **Self Assessment** |
| Amounts / complexity | Follow current GOV.UK guidance; higher or complex claims may need a return |

Always use the **current** GOV.UK instructions for P87 and Self Assessment — processes and thresholds can change.

---

## Keep good records

HMRC can ask for evidence. Keep:

- A **mileage log** (date, from, to, business purpose, miles) — use the pack’s log sheet
- Notes of **employer payments** (payslips, expense reports, mileage policy)
- Enough detail to show journeys were **business**, not private or ordinary commuting
