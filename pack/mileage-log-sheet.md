## HMRC Approved Mileage Allowance Payment (AMAP) rates — 2026/27

| Vehicle | First 10,000 business miles | Each mile over 10,000 |
|---------|----------------------------:|----------------------:|
| Cars and vans | **55p** | **25p** |
| Motorcycles | **24p** | **24p** |
| Bicycles | **20p** | **20p** |
| Passenger (cars/vans, per fellow employee) | **5p** per mile | **5p** per mile |

Source: [GOV.UK — Travel mileage and fuel rates and allowances](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-travel-mileage-and-fuel-allowances/travel-mileage-and-fuel-rates-and-allowances).

### Tips for a usable log

- Record **each business journey** soon after you travel (date, places, reason, miles).
- Ordinary commuting (home ↔ permanent workplace) is usually **not** business mileage — check GOV.UK.
- Keep employer mileage payment records so you can work out any **Mileage Allowance Relief** shortfall.
- Print this page as often as you need (A4). Add more blank sheets for longer years.
