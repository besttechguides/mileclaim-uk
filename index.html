Frequently asked questions

What are the HMRC mileage rates for 2026/27?

For tax year 2026/27 this calculator uses Approved Mileage Allowance Payment (AMAP) rates of 55p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a car or van, then 25p; 24p for motorcycles; 20p for bicycles. Passenger payments for cars and vans are +5p per passenger per business mile. Always verify current figures on GOV.UK: Travel — mileage and fuel rates and allowances.

What is Mileage Allowance Relief (MAR)?

If your employer reimburses you at less than the AMAP rate for the same business miles, the difference may qualify as Mileage Allowance Relief. Passenger shortfalls do not qualify for MAR. See Tax relief for employees — vehicles you use for work on GOV.UK. This tool estimates a shortfall only — it does not file a claim.

How do I keep a mileage log for HMRC?

Record each business journey with date, from, to, purpose and miles. Use the trip log above to store journeys in this browser, then download CSV or print. Keep records in case HMRC asks for evidence. MileClaim UK is an unofficial helper, not a filing service.

What does AMAP 55p mean?

AMAP is the Approved Mileage Allowance Payment. For 2026/27 the first-band car and van rate used here is 55p per business mile (45p in 2025/26). After 10,000 business miles in the tax year the car/van rate is 25p. Confirm on GOV.UK before you claim.
