Sole trader mileage checklist (2026/27)

Unofficial helper — not tax advice. Verify rates on GOV.UK before you claim.

Before you claim

  • Confirm you are using **simplified mileage (AMAP)** or actual costs (not both for the same vehicle for the same period in ways HMRC disallows — check GOV.UK)
  • Tax year dates correct (6 April–5 April)
  • Business vs private miles separated

For each business journey, log

  • Date
  • From / to
  • Purpose (business reason)
  • Miles
  • Vehicle type (car/van, motorcycle, bicycle)
  • Passengers (if claiming +5p)

Rates used on MileClaim UK (check GOV.UK)

- Cars & vans: **55p** first 10,000 business miles, then **25p**

- Motorcycles: **24p**

- Bicycles: **20p**

- Passenger: **+5p** per passenger per business mile
